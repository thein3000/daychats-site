One day, one thread.

Write a line now and another later. Browse past days or search for a thought you want to find again.

Just on your phone.

No account, no servers, no analytics. Your entries stay in the app until you choose to export them.

Yours to take with you.

Export a day, a date range, or your whole diary as Markdown. Plain text you can keep and read elsewhere.
